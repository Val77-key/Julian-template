About Larysa

Music has been at the heart of my life for many years. As a piano and music teacher, I believe that learning an instrument should be both meaningful and enjoyable. My goal is not simply to teach students how to play the notes, but to help them understand, feel, and express the music they create. I earned my Bachelor's Degree with Honors from Sumy State Music College in Ukraine and later completed a Doctorate Degree in Music at Sumy State Pedagogical Institute named after A.S. Makarenko.Over the years, I have taught students of different ages and abilities, worked with music theory and performance, coached students for regional and international competitions, directed choirs, and helped young musicians develop confidence in their musical abilities.

A personal approach to every student

Every student learns differently. That is why I adapt my lessons to each person's abilities, goals, interests, and pace of learning.Whether a student is discovering the piano for the first time, developing stronger technique, preparing for a performance, or simply learning music for enjoyment, I aim to create a supportive environment where they feel comfortable asking questions, making mistakes, and growing.I especially enjoy helping students discover pieces that inspire them. When students genuinely enjoy the music they are learning, practice becomes more meaningful and progress becomes something they can feel proud of.

More than learning to play

Piano lessons can develop much more than musical skills. They can encourage concentration, creativity, discipline, confidence, listening, and self-expression.My role as a teacher is to guide each student toward their own goals while helping them build a strong musical foundation that they can carry with them for years to come.

Let's make music something you look forward to.

Piano Lessons

Personalized piano instruction designed around each student's level, interests, and goals.Lessons can include piano technique, musical interpretation, sight-reading, scales, rhythm, repertoire, and performance skills. Beginners are welcome, and more experienced students can work toward developing greater confidence, musicality, and technical ability.

Lessons are available in person and online.

Music Theory

Understanding music makes playing an instrument more rewarding.Students can learn the fundamentals of music theory, including rhythm, pitch, tempo, chords, scales, notation, and musical structure. Lessons are adapted to the student's current knowledge and can range from the very basics to more advanced concepts.

Beginner Piano

Starting from scratch? That's absolutely okay.Beginner lessons focus on building a comfortable foundation while making the learning process enjoyable. Students gradually develop basic technique, note reading, rhythm, coordination, and musical understanding through age-appropriate and engaging pieces.

Technique & Musical Development

For students who already play piano, lessons can focus on strengthening technique and developing a deeper understanding of music.Depending on the student's needs, we may work on:

  • Piano technique and ergonomics
  • Scales and exercises
  • Sight-reading
  • Rhythm and musical phrasing
  • Repertoire development
  • Musical interpretation
  • Improvisation
  • Performance confidence

Performance Preparation

Preparing for a recital, competition, audition, or another important performance?Lessons can be tailored toward performance preparation, with attention to musical interpretation, technique, confidence, and presentation.Students can work through their repertoire while learning how to approach performance with greater preparation and confidence.

Personalized Instruction

Every student has different strengths, challenges, interests, and goals.Rather than following exactly the same approach with everyone, I adapt lessons to the individual student. Together, we can create a learning experience that is challenging enough to encourage progress while remaining positive and enjoyable.

Whether you're just beginning or continuing your musical journey, there is always something new to discover.

Interested in lessons? Get in touch to discuss your goals and find the right approach for you.
